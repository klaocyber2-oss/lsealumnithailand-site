Distinguished alumni

A few of the Thai LSE alumni who have shaped public life, scholarship and business. Dr Puey Ungphakorn was also made an Honorary Fellow of the School.

Dr Puey Ungphakorn

ดร.ป๋วย อึ๊งภากรณ์
Governor, Bank of Thailand, 1959–1971

Economist, central banker and educator, later Rector of Thammasat University. Received the Ramon Magsaysay Award for government service in 1965.

LSE: BSc and PhD Economics · Honorary Fellow of LSE, 1968

PhD

Dr Piyasvasti Amranand

ดร.ปิยสวัสดิ์ อัมระนันทน์
Minister of Energy, 2006–2008

Headed the National Energy Policy Office in the 1990s, then led Thai Airways as President from 2009 and later chaired PTT.

LSE: MSc and PhD Economics

Thirachai Phuvanatnaranubala

ธีระชัย ภูวนาถนรานุบาล
Minister of Finance, 2011–2012

A former Deputy Governor of the Bank of Thailand, he led the Securities and Exchange Commission as Secretary-General from 2003 to 2011.

LSE: BSc Economics, 1974

Suchart Chaovisith

สุชาติ เชาว์วิศิษฐ
Deputy Prime Minister, 2004

A career finance official who became Director-General of the Revenue Department, then Minister of Finance from 2003 to 2004.

LSE: Economics, 1962
PhD
2002

Prof Thitinan Pongsudhirak

ฐิตินันท์ พงษ์สุทธิรักษ์
Professor of International Relations, Chulalongkorn University

A regular Bangkok Post columnist and Visiting Professor at LSE's Saw Swee Hock Southeast Asia Centre. His LSE doctorate won the UK's best dissertation prize.

LSE: PhD International Relations, 2002
MSc

Panote Sirivadhanabhakdi

ปณต สิริวัฒนภักดี
Group CEO, Frasers Property, since 2016

Leads the real estate group and the One Bangkok development, and generously supported our 2026 roundtable with Professor Stefan Collignon.
