Membership

Open to everyone who studied at LSE and lives in, works in or has a close connection to Thailand.

Who can join

If you studied at LSE, you're welcome. We confirm this once, when you apply, so that our events stay a place for genuine alumni and their guests.

  • GraduatesUndergraduate, master's and doctoral programmes
  • Former studentsGeneral Course and other study-abroad students
  • Executive and short coursesParticipants in LSE executive education and certificate programmes

Eligibility is shown as drafted and will be confirmed by the committee.

How joining works

  1. ApplyFill in a short form with your name, contact details, programme and graduation year.
  2. We checkThe committee confirms that you studied at LSE, for example from your degree certificate or LSE alumni record.
  3. You're inWe welcome you to the association and send invitations to upcoming events.

What members get

  • Invitations to eventsGatherings, talks and trips through the year.
  • Guests welcomeBring a partner or friend to selected events; just register them with your booking.
  • A network that travelsLinks to LSE alumni groups across Asia and beyond.

Questions

Is there a membership fee?

The committee will publish fee details here. Joining the mailing list and following our events is free.

I'm not Thai. Can I still join?

Yes. Membership is about having studied at LSE and having a connection to Thailand, not nationality.

Can I bring a guest to events?

Yes, to most events. Register your guest when you sign up so we can plan numbers and venues.

How do you use my details?

Only to confirm your membership and to contact you about the association. We never sell or share them. See our privacy notice.

Ready to join?

The online application form opens soon. Until then, send us a message on Instagram and we'll add you to the list.
