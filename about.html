About the association

We are the LSE alumni community in Thailand: graduates of every programme and generation who studied in London and now build their lives and careers here. Connections beyond graduation, here in Bangkok.

Rerum cognoscere causas.

To know the causes of things. LSE's motto, and still a good reason to get together.

LSE counts 1,192 alumni in Thailand (LSE Alumni Relations, November 2026). They work across government, business, law, finance, academia, the media and civil society, and many came home with friendships and habits of mind they did not want to lose.

The association keeps those going. We host dinners and talks with LSE faculty visiting Bangkok, hold socials through the year, welcome each year's offer holders at LSE Connect, and work with LSE networks such as LSE Generate and with alumni groups across the region.

What we set out to do

Connect

Bring LSE alumni in Thailand together, across cohorts, programmes and professions, and with the School itself.

Think

Create room for ideas and debate through talks, roundtables and visits by LSE faculty.

Give back

Welcome future students, support new graduates, and represent the LSE community in Thailand and the region.

ดร.ป๋วย อึ๊งภากรณ์
Governor, Bank of Thailand, 1959–1971

Economist, central banker and educator, later Rector of Thammasat University. Received the Ramon Magsaysay Award for government service in 1965.
