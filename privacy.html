Privacy notice

How the LSE Alumni Association Thailand handles personal data, in line with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019).

What we collect

When you apply for membership or register for an event, we collect your name, email address, phone number, LSE programme and graduation year, and, for events, the names of any guests you bring.

Why we collect it

  • to confirm that you studied at LSE;
  • to send you invitations and news about the association;
  • to organise events, including guest lists and venue bookings.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone outside the committee, except a venue that needs a guest list for an event you have registered for.

Photographs

We sometimes take photos at events and share them on this website and on Instagram. If you would rather not appear, tell the photographer on the day or message us, and we will remove the photo.

How long we keep it

For as long as you are a member. If you leave, we delete your details within a reasonable period, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time by contacting the committee via Instagram.

Last updated 25 November 2026.
